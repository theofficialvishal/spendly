Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect information you provide directly to us, including your account details (username, email address) and financial logs such as expense entries, amounts, dates, descriptions, and categories.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide, maintain, and improve personal expense tracking features for you, such as displaying spending summaries, category breakdowns, and transaction history. We do not sell or rent your personal financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

We store your account and expense records securely. We employ standard technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your private financial data with external advertising networks or data brokers. We may rely on secure infrastructure providers solely to host the application and database.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app.
